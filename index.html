ThreadSnap: Thread Exporter

Save a long X / Twitter thread as a clean PDF or images.

A Chrome extension that scrolls an X / Twitter thread, captures every post, and stitches them into a PDF or image files — or captures just the posts you list by link or ID. All processed locally in your browser, with nothing collected or uploaded.
